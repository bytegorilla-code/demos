Setup checklist — do this BEFORE sending (not part of the email)
1. Domain email only. you@yourdomain.co.uk — never Gmail/Yahoo. Cold email from a free address gets filtered or distrusted instantly.
2. Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain DNS. Without these, UK business mail servers are far more likely to bin you. Your host can do this in ~20 minutes.
3. Warm up the domain. If it's new, send 10–15/day for the first two weeks, then increase gradually. Blasting 200 on day one is the fastest way to get blacklisted.
4. No attachments. One link. Attachments are the single biggest spam trigger in cold email — and recipients won't open them from strangers anyway.
5. Personalise line 2 genuinely. The "[ONE REAL OBSERVATION]" must be real — read their reviews. Generic = deleted.
6. Keep the unsubscribe line. Under UK PECR/GDPR you must offer opt-out and honour it immediately. Note: sole traders and partnerships need consent — corporate (Ltd) addresses are the safer target.
7. One follow-up only, 4–5 days later, then stop. More than that damages your domain reputation.
